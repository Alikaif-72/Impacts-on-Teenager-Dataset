Finding #01
Anxiety Drives MH Score
Anxiety level has the highest correlation (r=0.59) with mental health score, stronger than even stress level.
Finding #02
TikTok → Higher Depression
TikTok users show 3.0% depression rate vs. 2.3% for "Both" platform users — the highest among all groups.
Finding #03
Poor Sleep Tanks Wellness
Poor sleepers average 2.79 wellness score vs. 3.98 for good sleepers — a 43% gap in wellbeing.
Finding #04
Age 14 Most Stressed
14-year-olds have the highest stress level (6.18/10), while 15-year-olds report the highest anxiety (5.92/10).
